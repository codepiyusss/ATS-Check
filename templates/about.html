About ATS-Check

Why it exists

ATS-Check began as a student project to understand how resumes are read before a recruiter sees them. The goal is honest, explainable feedback you can act on, not a mystery score.

What an ATS does

Many employers use an applicant tracking system to read, sort and search resumes. Clear structure, readable text and relevant keywords generally help. Every company configures its system differently, so no tool, including this one, can tell you your exact score.

How the estimate works today

Your PDF is converted to text, then checked for common keywords, formatting signals (bullets, contact details, headings) and length. A more detailed, field-specific engine is being built, and this page will explain exactly how it scores when it's ready.
